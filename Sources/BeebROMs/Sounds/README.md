# Disc drive sounds

MAME's recordings of 5.25" and 3.5" floppy drives (`525_*`, `35_*`): the
motor starting, running and stopping, with and without a disc, a head step,
and seeks at 2, 6, 12 and 20ms a step. From
https://github.com/mamedev/mame/tree/master/samples/floppy, by way of b2,
under CC0 1.0 (see LICENSE).
